What Fillr does

Simple tools for real vehicle costs.

Fuel logging

Record litres, price, odometer and fuel type so every fill-up builds a clearer picture of your running costs.

Receipt scanning

Take a photo of a receipt and Fillr helps extract the important details automatically.

Trip tracking

Track trips for more accurate fuel usage reports and better tax-time records.

Fuel deals

Search fuel prices nearby or along your route to help find better value before you fill up.

Vehicle profiles

Manage multiple vehicles and keep each vehicle’s fuel history separate.

Tax-ready exports

Keep organised vehicle records that can be exported when it is time to prepare tax information.
