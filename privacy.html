Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

Fillr is designed to help users track fuel purchases, receipts, trips and vehicle running costs.

Your Data

Your data belongs to you. Fillr does not sell user information and is designed to give users control over their records.

How information is used

We use this information to provide app features, improve accuracy, support account functionality and help generate vehicle records.

Receipt and trip data

Receipt and trip information may include purchase locations, dates, fuel amounts, odometer readings and route-related records depending on the features you use.

Data storage

We take reasonable steps to protect user information. As Fillr grows, this policy will be updated to clearly explain where data is stored and how it is protected.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the support page.
